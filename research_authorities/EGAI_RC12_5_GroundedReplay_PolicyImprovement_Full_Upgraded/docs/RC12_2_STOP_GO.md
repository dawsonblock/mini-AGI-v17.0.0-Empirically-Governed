# RC12.2 Stop/Go Gates

## Adapters
GO only after at least 50 verified repeated gaps across at least 20 independent tasks, retrieval/procedure/composition failure, a demonstrated representation bottleneck, verified frozen base, and predicted transfer minus interference >= 0.02. These are engineering defaults, not universal constants.

## Learned plasticity
Remain OFF until >=1000 independently qualified outcome records with >=100 promoted interventions and offline superiority to deterministic routing.

## Model-based imagination
Remain proposal-only. Imagined outcomes never enter authoritative evidence. Enable planning experiments only after grounded replay calibration and OOD tests.

## Consolidation/shared weights
Require broad R0-R6 gains, zero security regression, preregistered foundation-retention tolerance, positive causal ablation, and rollback rehearsal.

## Architecture search
Remain OFF until controlled experiments isolate a substrate bottleneck rather than retrieval, routing, memory, skill, compute, or data quality failure.
